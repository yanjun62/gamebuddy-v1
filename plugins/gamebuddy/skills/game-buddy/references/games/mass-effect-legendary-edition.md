# Mass Effect Legendary Edition — LE2 / LE3 companion guide

## Scope

This public profile supports **Mass Effect 2** and **Mass Effect 3** in Legendary Edition. It is a spoiler-aware companion for terminology, on-screen orientation, squad/build discussion, and decision framing—not a completion walkthrough. It does not cover LE1, so do not silently reuse LE2/LE3 translations or story context for the first game.

The release includes a compact, hand-curated recognition list for common names, factions, locations, and systems. A larger 3,205-entry bilingual localization dataset used during private development is intentionally **not** bundled, because it derives from localization resources and is outside this package’s AGPL license.

## Player-state checklist

- Active title: LE2 or LE3.
- Visible objective, location, current party, and any prompt now on screen.
- Imported save or default world state; never infer prior choices.
- Goal: blind story, navigation, build help, loyalty/relationship routing, completion, achievement, or terminology lookup.
- Spoiler level: safe, current-game, or full.

## Spoiler boundaries

### Safe

- Discuss the current screen, mechanics already introduced, and facts the player supplies.
- Identify people, places, factions, and UI terms after they appear.
- Do not identify future squadmates, hidden allegiances, loyalty outcomes, deaths, or endgame conditions.

### Current game

- Discuss content the player has already reached in the active title.
- Hide later missions, imported-save consequences, and outcomes in the other title.
- Before explaining an irreversible decision, say that it can affect later content and ask whether the player wants the exact consequence.

### Full

- Reveal hidden outcomes or cross-title consequences only after explicit permission.

## Low-risk companion behavior

- Put the immediate, visible next action first. If the objective text is absent, ask for the journal or map instead of guessing a route.
- For builds, start from the player’s class, difficulty, preferred weapon/power style, and current abilities. Distinguish solid general advice from version-specific numbers.
- For a choice, compare what the prompt visibly says and flag that unseen consequences may exist. Ask before explaining them.
- Treat imported state, morality alignment, romance, and squad composition as player choices—not a canonical path.
- In overlay mode, keep the answer to one to three compact lines while preserving the agent's established voice. In guide mode, include prerequisites and risks after the immediate recommendation.

## Terminology workflow

- Keep the compact stt.terms list active for local speech recognition.
- Prefer the player’s on-screen subtitle and installed localization whenever it differs from this profile.
- If the player supplies a separately permitted local glossary, query only the 10–30 entries relevant to the current utterance or screen.
- Do not turn uncertain spelling matches into lore claims, and do not use generic credit strings as game terms.

## Known limits

- LE1 terms and walkthrough content are not included.
- This companion intentionally avoids exact mission cutoffs, achievement requirements, and hidden outcome routes until those claims have a cited guide pass.
- The profile’s Chinese names are speech-recognition hints, not a replacement for a player’s chosen localization mod.
