# Credits and data provenance

Game Buddy stores compact recognition data and user-authored guidance. All game text, names, worlds, and trademarks remain the property of their respective rightsholders. Do not present this project as an official localization, Wiki mirror, or replacement for the games.

## Disco Elysium

- **Game and original writing:** ZA/UM and the credited creators of *Disco Elysium*.
- **Simplified Chinese localization:** thanks to **轻语游戏（WhisperGames）** and the Chinese localization contributors. ZA/UM's 2020 Chinese-support announcement explicitly thanked WhisperGames for localization support; see the [archived Chinese announcement](https://www.vgbaike.com/disco_elysium/item17043).
- **Public-release boundary:** a private development build used a small actor-name mapping aligned from a legally installed game. That derived dataset is not included in this archive; no dialogue, subtitles, screenshots, maps, or game files are redistributed here.
- **Wiki verification:** [Disco Elysium Wiki](https://discoelysium.wiki.gg/) and its cited in-game dialogue records, especially the pages for [Fairweather T-500 armour](https://discoelysium.wiki.gg/wiki/Fairweather_T-500_/_VE_Armour), [Find all armour pieces](https://discoelysium.wiki.gg/wiki/Find_all_armour_pieces), and [Dresscode](https://discoelysium.wiki.gg/wiki/Dresscode).
- **Chinese guide synthesis:** the user-supplied composite guide credits GCORES world notes, Zhihu analyses, A9VG platinum guidance, Douban skill-name comparisons, Bilibili walkthroughs, Steam achievement guides, and other community research. Exact individual article links were not included in the supplied document, so Game Buddy credits the source communities without inventing author attribution.

## Mass Effect Legendary Edition

- **Games and original writing:** BioWare, Electronic Arts, and the credited creators of the *Mass Effect* trilogy.
- **Simplified Chinese localization:** thanks to the maintainers and translators of [Simplified Chinese Localization on Nexus Mods](https://www.nexusmods.com/masseffectlegendaryedition/mods/1370). The accompanying community installation guide states that the project revises and proofreads earlier 3DM, 轩辕, and player translations; see the [Steam Community guide](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3068557832).
- **Public-release boundary:** a private development build used an LE2/LE3 localization-derived terminology dataset. The raw dataset is not included in this archive and is not relicensed under MIT. The published profile instead contains a small recognition-oriented term list.

## Use policy

- Preserve source links and localization credits when adapting or distributing derived profile data.
- Keep optional third-party glossaries separate from code and self-authored guide prose.
- Load only the active game’s profile, and retrieve only terms relevant to the current screen or utterance.
- Before publishing any new game-derived dataset, confirm its redistribution permission independently.
