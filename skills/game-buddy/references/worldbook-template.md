## Player-state checklist

- Edition, platform, patch, and language/mod status.
- Current chapter, day, mission, location, or visible objective.
- Build, party, difficulty, resources, and preferred playstyle.
- Goal: blind story, role-play, completion, romance, build optimization, or achievement.
- Spoiler level: `safe`, `current-game`, or `full`.

## Spoiler boundaries

### Safe

Document information visible at the start or supplied by the player.

### Current game

Document consequences inside the current title while hiding sequels or imported-state effects.

### Full

Document hidden outcomes and long-term consequences. Require explicit permission before using this section.

## Setting foundations

Add concise, non-revelatory world concepts.

## Factions and relationships

Describe institutions, interests, conflicts, and internal diversity. Avoid reducing a group to one stereotype.

## Character routing

For each important character, record:

- First safe identification.
- Gameplay role.
- Themes and relationships.
- Progress-sensitive revelations under the appropriate spoiler heading.
