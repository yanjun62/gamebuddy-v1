## Companion contract

This profile is for sitting beside the player, not replacing their playthrough.

- Default to `safe`: comment only on the current screen and facts the player has supplied.
- Use one to three compact lines in Overlay mode while preserving the agent's established voice. Do not turn every screenshot into a guide dump.
- Give the recommendation first in Guide mode. Mention prerequisites, risks, and missables only when relevant.
- Treat failed checks as alternate characterization and story paths, not automatically as mistakes.
- Ask before revealing a hidden outcome, culprit, late-game location, or irreversible consequence.
- Never use the Full-spoiler section unless the player explicitly chooses `full`.
- The Chinese names in this file follow the supplied Chinese composite guide. Use aliases from the profile when a localization differs.

## Player-state checklist

Before detailed guidance, establish only what is needed:

- Edition: original release or **The Final Cut**.
- Platform and language; note any translation or UI mod.

## Spoiler boundaries

### Safe

Safe at the beginning or after the player shows it:

- The protagonist wakes with severe amnesia in Martinaise and works a murder case.
- Kim Kitsuragi is the investigating partner assigned from another precinct.
- Skills are voices and perspectives as well as stats; higher is not always psychologically healthier.
- White checks can be reopened after a relevant skill increase or modifier. Red checks are one-shot branches.
- Failing can create excellent scenes and does not necessarily block completion.
- Revachol, Martinaise, the RCM, Wild Pines, the union dispute, and the Pale may be explained without predicting plot outcomes.

Do not name the culprit, describe the complete death mechanism, expose late-game locations, or reveal hidden character relationships.

### Current game

May discuss content the player has reached in this title, while hiding later consequences. Confirm the visible objective or day before using the routing sections below.

When a choice has a hidden permanent consequence, state only: “This can close or alter another route. Do you want the exact consequence?”

### Full

May reveal the culprit, complete evidence chain, tribunal outcomes, ending conditions, and all side-quest resolutions only after explicit permission such as “可以全剧透”.

## Immediate guidance

### First-hour priorities

1. Check health and morale before risky interactions; either reaching zero can end the run.
2. Explore the room and hotel before spending scarce skill points.
3. Keep one skill point unspent when possible. It can reopen an important white check during dialogue.
4. Swap clothing for a specific check when useful, but do not let optimization erase role-play.
5. Save outside dialogue before a red check only if the player wants save-scumming. For a blind narrative run, accept interesting failures.

### Check model

- Four attributes each govern six skills, for 24 skills total.
- A check uses two six-sided dice plus skill and modifiers against a difficulty target.
- A total roll of 2 is an automatic failure; 12 is an automatic success.
- Passive checks fire automatically and add observations or internal dialogue.
- White checks can be retried after they are unlocked again by skill growth or new modifiers.
- Red checks occur once and branch on the result.

The supplied composite guide lists example difficulty bands and exact target values. Treat those figures as secondary-source guidance until checked against the active edition or an original reference.

## Setting foundations

### Elysium, isolas, and the Pale

Elysium is a world of material regions called isolas separated by the Pale. The Pale is not ordinary empty space; it destabilizes matter, memory, and meaning. Travel and exposure are political, scientific, and psychological questions in the setting.

### Revachol and Martinaise

Revachol is a city marked by monarchy, revolution, foreign intervention, and continuing occupation. Martinaise is a neglected district where the RCM has limited authority and local power is divided among residents, the harbor union, and Wild Pines.

### RCM

The Revachol Citizens Militia is the protagonist’s institution. It performs police work without full sovereignty and carries the contradictions of the occupied city.

### Political vocabulary

The game tracks communist, fascist/traditionalist, moralist, and ultraliberal responses. These are not neutral build classes. Each is written as a political and psychological lens, usually with satire and self-critique.

## Mechanics and builds

### The four attributes

- **Intellect**: Logic, Encyclopedia, Rhetoric, Drama, Conceptualization, Visual Calculus.
- **Psyche**: Volition, Inland Empire, Empathy, Authority, Esprit de Corps, Suggestion.
- **Physique**: Endurance, Pain Threshold, Physical Instrument, Electrochemistry, Shivers, Half Light.
- **Motorics**: Hand/Eye Coordination, Perception, Reaction Speed, Savoir Faire, Interfacing, Composure.

### Build guidance

- For a first blind run, avoid treating any one recommended array as mandatory. A low stat creates a distinctive detective, not a broken save.
- A balanced 3/4/2/3 or 4/3/2/3 style array is a forgiving secondary-source suggestion, not a canonical optimum.
- Psyche-heavy play emphasizes empathy, intuition, authority, and internal life.
- Intellect-heavy play emphasizes deduction, history, rhetoric, and conceptual analysis.
- Shivers and Inland Empire are strong atmosphere choices; Perception reveals many environmental details.
- Volition protects morale, Endurance protects health, but consumables and clothing can compensate.
- Extremely high skills can become intrusive or misleading voices. Explain this as role-play texture, not a penalty to be eliminated.

### Thought Cabinet

- Skill points unlock thought slots as well as raise skills.
- Internalization takes in-game time; completed thoughts may grant mixed bonuses and drawbacks.
- Forgetting a thought costs a skill point and is permanent for that run.
- Keep advice contextual. Do not call one thought universally “best” without asking whether the player wants an XP-maximizing route.

The source brief highlights Actual Art Degree, Wompty-Dompty Dom Centre, White Mourning, and Lonesome Long Way Home-style optimization. English and Chinese thought names vary by localization; resolve against the player’s UI before giving exact instructions.

### Items, drugs, and clothing

- Clothing changes skills and can be swapped before checks.
- Drugs temporarily raise attributes and have health or morale costs.
- Tools must sometimes be equipped in a hand to expose an interaction.
- Selling everything is not automatically optimal; some objects matter to tasks, dialogue, or achievements.

### Key equipment and armour completionism

Use this section only when the player requests achievement/anti-missable help or has already encountered the corpse armour. In `safe` Overlay mode, reveal only the next actionable warning.

#### Fairweather T-500: first irreversible warning

- The achievement collectible consists of **four wearable pieces**: helmet, cuirass, gauntlets, and greaves/boots. The setting describes a fifth leggings component, but it is not collectible.
- After Cuno points toward **Call Me Mañana**, choose the dialogue intention to find **all** armour pieces. “Find one armour piece” and “Find all armour pieces” are mutually exclusive task routes.
- If the body is sent to Processing with the boots still attached, the full-set task cannot be opened or is cancelled. This is the earliest high-priority armour warning.
- Wearing all four collectible pieces together unlocks **Fairweather T-500 Vitreous Enamel** (“Suit up. Head to toe.”). The full-set task also grants 70 XP.

Verified against the [Find all armour pieces task page](https://discoelysium.wiki.gg/wiki/Find_all_armour_pieces) and [achievement list](https://discoelysium.wiki.gg/wiki/Achievements). The supplied appendix labels Call Me Mañana as “Mullen”; keep “马列拉” only as a possible Chinese-screen alias and do not use `Mullen` as the English name.

#### Greaves / boots

1. Finish the field autopsy first.
2. Return when Kim is not in the party; waiting until he goes to bed is the simplest reliable route.
3. Pull the boots off the body before it is sent to Processing.
4. Ask **Gorący Kubek** in the Whirling-in-Rags kitchen to boil and disinfect them, producing the wearable greaves.

The collectible greaves give **+2 Authority, −1 Composure**. See [Fairweather T-500 Boots with Rotting Flesh](https://discoelysium.wiki.gg/wiki/Fairweather_T-500_Boots_with_Rotting_Flesh). Do not advise fabricating a reason to send Kim to the ice-bear fridge when simply returning after bedtime is available.

#### Gauntlets

1. On Day 3+, follow the missing-armour dialogue toward **Cindy the SKULL**.
2. Cindy points to a little girl in the fishing village.
3. Ask **Little Lily** inside Lilienne’s house.
4. Retrieve the gauntlets from the sandcastle behind the house.

The gauntlets give **+2 Interfacing**. Location and route are documented in the [clothing table](https://discoelysium.wiki.gg/wiki/Dresscode) and [full-armour task](https://discoelysium.wiki.gg/wiki/Find_all_armour_pieces).

#### Cuirass

- Available from **Gary, the Cryptofascist**, on Day 3+.
- Obtain the Yellow-Man Mug from the Whirling-in-Rags trash container and confront Gary about it; then ask about the armour and pass the **Composure white check**. The task page lists this check as difficulty 14 with several discoverable modifiers.
- The cuirass can be recovered even if the full-armour task is not active.
- It gives **+1 Pain Threshold, +1 Volition, −1 Empathy** and can absorb a relevant incoming shot in the tribunal.
- Because +1 Volition effectively adds one Morale, taking the cuirass off while sitting at 1 Morale can cause game over.

See [Fairweather T-500 Cuirass](https://discoelysium.wiki.gg/wiki/Fairweather_T-500_Cuirass) and [Gary, the Cryptofascist](https://discoelysium.wiki.gg/wiki/Gary%2C_the_Cryptofascist).

#### Helmet — late-game spoiler guard

In `safe` or early `current-game`, say only: “The helmet is late-game and not available in Martinaise; I can tell you the exact location if you permit that spoiler.”

After full/current-progress permission: the helmet is in the Insulindian Phasmid’s nest at the Sea Fortress and becomes accessible after the creature leaves. It gives **+1 Half Light, −1 Suggestion**. Completing every earlier cryptozoology step is important for the richest encounter, but the supplied appendix overstates that chain as the sole condition for acquiring the helmet. See [Inspect the phasmid](https://discoelysium.wiki.gg/wiki/Inspect_the_phasmid) and [Fairweather T-500 armour](https://discoelysium.wiki.gg/wiki/Fairweather_T-500_/_VE_Armour).

## Full-spoiler reference

**LOCKED: use only after explicit full-spoiler permission.**

### Murder solution

The hanged mercenary was shot while with Klaasje. Klaasje and Ruby helped stage the hanging, and the Hardie Boys participated in the cover story. The shooter is the Deserter on the island, a surviving former communard whose ideology, isolation, jealousy, and deterioration overlap as motives.

Evidence linking the island includes ballistics, the line of fire, flowers, the absence of an audible nearby shot, and other material traces. Do not flatten the explanation into a single motive.

### Ruby

The confrontation can end with Ruby escaping or dying. Present dialogue/check requirements only when the player asks for an exact route, and verify against the active edition.

### Tribunal

The tribunal is a major forced confrontation after the Ruby route. Survival, Kim’s condition, and multiple character outcomes depend on prior equipment, checks, and choices. Do not promise a guaranteed result from one stat alone.

### Island and phasmid

The Deserter is found on the island. The Insulindian Phasmid may appear late if the associated route has been supported; Kim’s presence and a check affect whether the encounter can be documented. The creature’s statements support several interpretations and should not be presented as an uncontested objective cosmology.

### Ending evaluation

The final RCM debrief evaluates case progress, conduct, substance use, and partner outcome. A strong relationship with Kim and keeping him active can support recruiting him to the 41st, while Cuno can occupy a different partner route under other circumstances. Verify exact requirements before prescribing an ending.
