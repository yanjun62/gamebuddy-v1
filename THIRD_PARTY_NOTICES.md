## Bundled game guides

The Disco Elysium guide is a spoiler-aware, source-attributed synthesis. It includes no game dialogue, subtitles, screenshots, maps, save files, or extracted game bundles. Its sources and confidence limits are recorded in `skills/game-buddy/references/credits.md` and the guide itself.

The Mass Effect Legendary Edition guide in this archive is terminology-first for LE2 and LE3. It is not a full walkthrough and must not be presented as one. It includes a compact hand-curated recognition profile only.

## Excluded localization datasets

This public archive intentionally does **not** include the 424-entry Disco Elysium bilingual actor-name export or the 3,205-entry Mass Effect LE2/LE3 bilingual localization export used in the private development build. Those datasets derive from game/localization resources and are not covered by this archive’s AGPL license.

The archive retains optional import/extraction scripts so a user may create a local glossary only from material they are entitled to use and only in accordance with the applicable terms. Do not upload, sell, or claim ownership of third-party localization data.
